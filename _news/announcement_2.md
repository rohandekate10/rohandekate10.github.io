Presented research titled *"Multi-fidelity Learning with Stacked Deep Operator Networks and Conformalized Uncertainty Quantification"* at the Computational Interdisciplinary Graduate Programs (CIGP) Symposium, April 2024.

---

This work explores advanced techniques in scientific machine learning, focusing on how to effectively combine information from multiple data sources of varying fidelity levels. The research demonstrates how stacked deep operator networks can leverage both high-fidelity and low-fidelity data to improve model performance while maintaining computational efficiency.

Key contributions include:
- Development of a multi-fidelity learning framework using deep operator networks
- Integration of conformalized uncertainty quantification for robust predictions
- Application to challenging scientific computing problems

The presentation received positive feedback from the computational science community and opened up new collaboration opportunities.
