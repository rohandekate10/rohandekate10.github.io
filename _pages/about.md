I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Purdue University, working at the intersection of **Scientific Machine Learning**, **Computational Imaging**, and **Numerical Methods**. My research is supported by the **Eli Lilly and Company and Purdue University Research Alliance Center (LPRC)**, where I focus on developing advanced machine learning techniques for pharmaceutical applications.

## Research Focus

My research interests span several key areas:

- **Scientific Machine Learning**: Developing deep operator learning frameworks for solving partial differential equations and modeling complex physical systems
- **Computational Imaging**: Applying machine learning to enhance imaging techniques for pharmaceutical and medical applications

## Background

Prior to my PhD, I completed my **Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering (MSME)** at Purdue University with a concentration in Computational Science & Engineering. My master's thesis focused on *"Multi-level Deep Operator Learning with Applications to Distributional Shift, Uncertainty Quantification, and Multi-fidelity Learning"* under the guidance of Dr. Guang Lin.

I also bring extensive industry experience from my time at **Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd.** and **Stellantis**, where I worked on emission control systems, vehicle powertrain optimization, and software development for hybrid transmission controls. This practical experience informs my research approach, ensuring that my work addresses real-world engineering challenges.
